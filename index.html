Standing rules

Tick means all sets, clean. If the last set turned into a grind or the form broke, leave it unticked and repeat the weight. The bar doesn't care what you meant to lift.

Lower body override: if it feels off on any given day, that session reverts to light technique work regardless of the phase. Progress data beats the schedule.

Home days: 5–10 minutes. Pull-ups and push-ups stopping 1–2 reps short of failure, plus hip, shoulder and ankle mobility. Sore the next day means you did too much.
